Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about GYMFIT

Membership & Pricing

What membership plans are available? +

We offer three membership plans:

  • Basic Plan (₹999/month): Gym access, cardio training, weight training
  • Standard Plan (₹1,999/month): All basic features + weight loss program + nutrition guide
  • Premium Plan (₹3,999/month): All features + personal trainer + full diet plan + weekly progress checks
Can I change my membership plan? +

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your membership plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Simply visit our front desk or contact our membership team.

Is there a joining fee? +

We occasionally offer special promotions with no joining fee. Standard joining fee is ₹999, which includes your initial fitness assessment and gym orientation. Ask about our current promotions!

Can I freeze my membership? +

Yes, you can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per year for medical reasons, travel, or other valid circumstances. A nominal freeze fee of ₹299 per month applies.

Facilities & Equipment

What equipment is available? +

Our gym is fully equipped with:

  • Cardio machines: treadmills, ellipticals, stationary bikes, rowing machines
  • Strength training: free weights, squat racks, bench presses
  • Resistance machines for all muscle groups
  • Functional training area with battle ropes, kettlebells, TRX
  • Yoga and stretching area with mats and props
What are the gym hours? +

We're open 7 days a week:

  • Monday - Friday: 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Saturday: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Sunday: 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM

Holiday hours may vary - check our notice board or app for updates.

Is parking available? +

Yes, we have dedicated parking for our members with 50+ spaces available. Parking is free for all members during gym hours. Additional street parking is also available nearby.

Are there changing rooms and showers? +

Yes! We have clean, modern changing rooms with lockers, showers, and complimentary toiletries. Towel service is available for premium members.

Training & Classes

Do I need to book classes in advance? +

While some classes accept walk-ins, we recommend booking in advance to secure your spot, especially for popular classes like HIIT and spinning. You can book through our mobile app or at the front desk.

What types of group classes are offered? +

We offer a variety of group fitness classes:

  • Yoga and Pilates
  • HIIT and Circuit Training
  • Spinning and Cardio
  • Strength and Conditioning
  • Dance Fitness
  • Boxing and Martial Arts
How do I get started with personal training? +

Getting started is easy! Schedule a free consultation with one of our certified trainers. They'll assess your fitness level, discuss your goals, and create a personalized training plan. Personal training sessions are available in packages of 5, 10, or 20 sessions.

Are beginners welcome in all classes? +

Absolutely! We offer classes for all fitness levels. Each class description indicates the difficulty level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or All Levels). Our instructors are trained to provide modifications for different fitness levels.

Health & Safety

Do I need a medical clearance? +

If you have pre-existing medical conditions, are over 65, or have been inactive for more than 6 months, we recommend consulting your doctor before starting any exercise program. Our trainers can also help assess your readiness for exercise.

How is the gym cleaned and maintained? +

We maintain high standards of cleanliness with:

  • Daily professional cleaning
  • Hand sanitizer stations throughout the gym
  • Disinfectant wipes for equipment
  • Regular equipment maintenance checks
  • Clean towels available for all members
What should I bring to the gym? +

Bring comfortable workout clothes, proper athletic shoes, a water bottle, and a lock for the locker. We provide towels, water fountains, and all necessary equipment. Don't forget your membership card!
